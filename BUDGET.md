# 💸 BUDGET — what this costs, and the free path

The plan is nearly free until Stage 13. Everything below has a free option; paid items
buy speed or polish, not access. **Set a billing alarm before creating any cloud account.**

| Stage | Free path | Optional paid | Typical cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00–04 | CS50, py4e, Automate the Boring Stuff, Exercism, Missing Semester, Pro Git, Bandit | Python Crash Course, pytest book | $0–60 |
| 03 | NeetCode, LeetCode free, Tech Interview Handbook | LeetCode Premium (locked design/concurrency problems, company tags) | $0 / ~$35/mo from Stage 09 |
| 05–06 | Local Postgres, SQLBolt, pgexercises, FastAPI docs, cosmicpython | Learning SQL, TestDriven.io course, DDIA | $0–120 |
| 06 | Deploy: Fly.io / Render / Railway free tiers, or a ~$5/mo VPS | | $0–5/mo |
| 07–10 | All free (HPBN, Beej, OSTEP, Little Book of Semaphores, Redis University, AWS Builders' Library) | Fowler asyncio book, Release It! | $0–80 |
| 11–12 | System Design Primer, Hello Interview free guides, MIT 6.824, Kleppmann lectures, Gossip Glomers | Alex Xu vol 1–2, DDIA, Database Internals, paid mocks | $0–150 + mocks ($100–200 each, optional) |
| 13 | kind/minikube locally, killercoda, free tiers (AWS/GCP), GitHub Actions free minutes | Kubernetes Up & Running, KodeKloud, a small cloud bill for a week of experiments | $0–50 cloud (**alarm at $20**) |
| 14–16 | Colab / Kaggle free GPUs, Karpathy, d2l.ai, fast.ai | Raschka's LLM book, Colab Pro (~$10/mo) | $0–60 |
| 17–19 | Colab, small open models on CPU/T4, vLLM on Colab | Renting 1–2 GPUs by the hour (Lambda/RunPod/Vast: ~$0.5–3/GPU-hr; budget ~20 hours total) | $0–60 |
| 20 | Peer mocks (Pramp, Discord), AI-as-interviewer | Paid mocks with real engineers (Hello Interview, interviewing.io) | $0–600 |

**Free-only total:** $0 plus a few dollars of cloud. **Comfortable total over the whole
road:** roughly $400–900 in books, one or two subscriptions, and a handful of paid mocks.

Rules:
- Never pay for a course before finishing its free equivalent's first chapter.
- Cloud: alarm at $20, budget cap at $50, destroy everything with `terraform destroy` when
  done, never leave a GPU instance running overnight.
- Books: libraries, used copies, and the free online editions listed in `RESOURCES.md`.
