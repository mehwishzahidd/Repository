# Story bank (STAR: Situation · Task · Action · Result · what I learned)

Two to three stories per prompt by Stage 20. Non-tech jobs count: ops, retail, warehouse
and school all contain real prioritisation, pushback and ownership stories. Revise this
file at the end of every stage. Each story should fit in 2 minutes and in 5.

## Prompts
1. Hardest technical project
2. An architecture decision and its trade-off
3. A scaling challenge
4. A hard bug and the signals I used to find it
5. A project that failed and what I changed
6. A technical disagreement and how I handled it
7. Prioritising under pressure
8. Working through ambiguity
9. Ownership: something I took responsibility for
10. An ethical / safety call (privacy, user harm, over-collecting data)
11. Choosing the simpler solution over the clever one
12. Pushing back on something I thought was wrong
