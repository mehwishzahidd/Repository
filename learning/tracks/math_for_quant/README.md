# Track 6 — Math for Quant

> Quant trading is **applied mathematics**. This track builds the math foundation that the
> Quant Trading track (Track 7) depends on. The good news: you can chip away at it **in
> parallel** with your coding tracks, a little at a time.

**Prerequisite:** Comfort with high-school algebra. Everything else is built up here.

---

## Why it matters for your goals
Quant interviews and the job itself lean heavily on **probability, statistics, and linear
algebra**. Strong math is the single biggest differentiator for quant roles — more than
fancy programming. Start early because math maturity takes time to develop.

---

## Syllabus (in priority order for quant)

### Part A — Probability (the most important by far)
1. **Counting & combinatorics** — permutations, combinations, the multiplication rule
2. **Basic probability** — sample spaces, conditional probability, independence
3. **Bayes' theorem** — updating beliefs with evidence (a quant-interview favorite)
4. **Random variables** — discrete & continuous, PMF/PDF/CDF
5. **Expectation & variance** — `E[X]`, `Var(X)`, linearity of expectation (used constantly)
6. **Common distributions** — Bernoulli, binomial, geometric, Poisson, normal
7. **Expected value puzzles** — the bread and butter of quant brainteasers

### Part B — Statistics
8. **Descriptive stats** — mean, median, variance, covariance, correlation
9. **Estimation** — sampling, confidence intervals, maximum likelihood
10. **Hypothesis testing** — p-values, t-tests
11. **Regression** — linear regression (also the gateway to ML)

### Part C — Linear algebra
12. **Vectors & matrices** — operations, dot products
13. **Matrix algebra** — inverses, determinants, solving linear systems
14. **Eigenvalues & eigenvectors** — PCA, covariance matrices
15. **Applications** — portfolios, covariance, dimensionality reduction

### Part D — Calculus (lighter, as needed)
16. **Derivatives & optimization** — finding maxima/minima (used in modeling)
17. **Integrals** — areas under curves, expectation of continuous variables
18. **A taste of stochastic calculus** — Brownian motion, the idea behind Black-Scholes (advanced)

## Free resources
- **Khan Academy** — probability, statistics, linear algebra, calculus (best free, from scratch)
- **3Blue1Brown** (YouTube) — "Essence of Linear Algebra" & "Essence of Calculus" — build
  deep *intuition* with gorgeous visuals
- **"Introduction to Probability"** by Blitzstein & Hwang (free PDF + Harvard's Stat 110
  lectures on YouTube) — the gold standard for probability
- **MIT OpenCourseWare** — 18.06 (Linear Algebra) by Gilbert Strang
